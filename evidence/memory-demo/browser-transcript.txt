In the context of a Lending Market (such as a decentralized finance protocol or a peer-to-peer lending platform), the field **`rates`** typically refers to the **interest rate(s)** associated with the loan or the lending pool.

Specifically, it usually represents:

1.  **The Borrowing Interest Rate**: The annual percentage rate (APR) or annual percentage yield (APY) that borrowers must pay to borrow assets from the pool.
2.  **The Lending Interest Rate**: The annual percentage yield (APY) that lenders earn by supplying assets to the pool.
